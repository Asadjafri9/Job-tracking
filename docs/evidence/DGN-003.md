## Scope delivered

A static HTML/CSS/JavaScript prototype makes the DESIGN_BRIEF section 10 journeys selectable without wiring them into `apps/web` or the extension. `README.md` lists direct hash routes and explains the local review process. The source uses DGN-002 semantic tokens and the proposed Guiding A assets. Proposed brand and identity remain provisional.

The prototype includes sign-in/recovery and all four onboarding steps; empty and returning Home; profile summary, document parse candidates requiring explicit per-fact confirmation, and provider choices; all seven extension states; application list/detail/evidence; email draft/review/handoff/explicit user-send confirmation; source setup/digest/partial/failure/paused; notification inbox/empty/error/permission-revoked; privacy, export review, and deletion review.

The experience stays progressive: each main section has one “Explore view” selector, setup has four linked steps, and the extension has named stages. At narrow widths Home collapses to compact navigation; the extension remains a separate narrow panel. A global sample banner and contextual copy identify illustrative states. The email confirmation preview remains disabled until the sample “I confirm” checkbox is checked. Provider setup, source preview, and attempted/fill/sent outcomes are not live.

## Limitations and gates

All people, records, URLs, times, statuses, and job matches are examples. There is no backend, persistence, identity, upload/parsing, provider call, Gmail tab, clipboard operation, public-source fetch, extension content-script, employer-form write, submit, notification delivery, export, or deletion. A navigation to a sample completion/attempt state is labeled as preview and does not save an event. The renderer uses a local temporary Chrome profile and no external services.

This is not the DGN-004 five-person usability study, and it does not complete DGN-005 keyboard/screen-reader, focus, reduced-motion, zoom, target-size, or full accessibility handoff. The listed results establish rendered coverage only; they do not establish production behavior, brand approval, or user validation.
