## Work completed

Added a skip-to-main-content link, visible keyboard focus, focus transfer to each new route heading, 44px minimum heights for core controls, a persistent associated label for the privacy appearance selector, and Rolevo design/identity guidance. Extension-panel controls and focus indicators meet the same 44px design target. Reduced-motion preferences are honored.

The web/extension product applications remain prototypes and are not fully implemented. This is a design handoff and prototype audit, not production readiness or accessibility certification.

## Limits

No VoiceOver/NVDA session or human assistive-technology review was performed. Automated DOM semantics and visible keyboard navigation do not establish actual spoken output or every screen-reader interaction. Contrast was checked for the documented design tokens; the full rendered prototype was not pixel-sampled for every text/background combination. The 320px layout check is a reflow proxy, not a browser zoom session. No job-seeker usability study or production-screen sign-off is claimed. These are recorded as limits; the M0 design handoff acceptance is based on the recorded code/semantic/layout checks.
