## Scope and manual review

The design brief was clarified to follow the contracts.

Added ADR-011 to `docs/DECISIONS.md` to separate user-confirmed scope from plan defaults and record the following unresolved owner decisions:

- Acceptance of the proposed Aster name, Guiding A logo, tagline, Sage & ink palette, and theme policy. Public brand use remains separately gated by DGN-006.
- Whether Microsoft Entra External ID is the selected identity product.
- Exact provider model IDs and allowed routing after availability, capabilities, and vendor privacy terms are verified.
- Whether age/DOB has a supported product purpose and should appear in the UI.
- Processing region and final retention/privacy disclosures.
- Azure region, service SKUs, and budget/spend approval. Selecting Azure as the stack does not authorize provisioning or spending.
