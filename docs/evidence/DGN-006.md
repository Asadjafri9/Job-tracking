# DGN-006 preliminary brand research and owner decision blocker

Date: October 12, 2026
Task state: BLOCKED — owner decision pending
Reviewer: Codex (preliminary research only)

## Preliminary findings

- The proposed product name is **Aster**. A public hiring-automation product brands itself “Aster” and describes applicant tracking, resume parsing, and hiring workflows at [hireaster.com](https://hireaster.com/product/automation). This is an adjacent commercial category and a reason to pause public use while the owner decides whether to rename or seek professional clearance. This observation is not a legal conclusion that the names conflict.
- Verisign RDAP returned registered records for [aster.com](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/aster.com) and [hireaster.com](https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/hireaster.com), last updated October 12, 2026. These exact domains are not available based on those records; other extensions and naming options have not been exhaustively checked.
- The [USPTO's federal trademark search guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/federal-trademark-searching) says clearance can be complex and considers similarity plus related goods/services; it recommends a broader, multi-query search and notes a search cannot guarantee registration. This review did not run a comprehensive federal or international trademark search, inspect every live/pending mark, search all design/logo codes, or obtain legal advice.

## Required owner action

The Aster name, Guiding A mark, tagline, and palette remain proposals. The owner must decide whether to retain the proposal for internal development only, rename, or obtain suitable professional trademark clearance before public brand use. Record the decision and any resulting rename work here. Until then, no public brand clearance is claimed and DGN-006 remains BLOCKED.
